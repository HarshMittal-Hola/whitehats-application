### 3. Recent Cybersecurity Incident
* **Incident Name/Target:** The October 2026 French Supermarket Refrigeration Attacks.
* **What Happened:** Hackers exploited internet-exposed central control panels managing cooling units across hundreds of French supermarkets, delaying temperature alarms by altering defrost cycles. The attack forced major retailers to dump vast amounts of frozen food to protect consumer safety, resulting in losses totaling tens of millions of euros. 
* **My Takeaway:** This incident highlights the critical dangers of the Internet of Things (IoT) and supply chain vulnerabilities.
